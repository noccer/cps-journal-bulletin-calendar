Using Google Calendar on mobile?

Google Calendar on phones and tablets does not reliably let you subscribe to a public calendar directly in the app.

Copy this link, then on a computer open Google Calendar > Other calendars > From URL, paste the link, and choose Add calendar. It will then appear on your phone as well.

How it works

  • Le Journal and Le Bulletin are checked for school dates.
  • The dates are added to a separate calendar for parents to use.
  • You add that calendar once, and it keeps itself up to date.
