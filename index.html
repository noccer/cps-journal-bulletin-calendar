Camberwell Primary bulletin calendar

Le Journal can be long, and important dates are easy to miss. This unofficial calendar picks out school dates from Le Journal and the shorter Le Bulletin emails so your calendar app can show them for you.

Please subscribe to the calendar link. Do not download or import the ICS file, because that creates a one-off copy that will not update.

Add it to your calendar

Choose the option that matches the calendar app you use. If a button opens a sign-in page, sign in and confirm the calendar subscription.

Calendar apps refresh subscribed calendars on their own schedule, usually every few hours. New dates may not appear instantly.

How it works

  • Long Le Journal emails and short Monday bulletin emails are checked for school dates.
  • Dates found in those emails are added to a separate calendar automatically.
  • You subscribe to that calendar, and your calendar app refreshes it on its own schedule.

Independent project

This project is not associated with Camberwell Primary School. The calendar is generated from bulletin dates and may contain mistakes or omissions.
