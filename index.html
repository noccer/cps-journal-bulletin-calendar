Calendar subscription

Le Journal and Le Bulletin dates, added to your calendar automatically

Le Journal can be long, and important dates are easy to miss. This unofficial calendar picks out school dates from Le Journal and the shorter Le Bulletin emails so your calendar app can show them for you.

Please subscribe to the calendar link. Do not download or import the ICS file, because that creates a one-off copy that will not update.

What it looks like

Once subscribed, school dates show up inside your normal calendar view. Here is a real example in Google Calendar month and week view.

How it works

  • Le Journal newsletters and Monday bulletin emails are checked for school dates.
  • Dates found in both of those updates are added to a separate calendar automatically.
  • You subscribe to that calendar, and your calendar app refreshes it on its own schedule.

About

This is an Independent project

This project is not associated with Camberwell Primary School. The calendar is generated from bulletin dates and may contain mistakes or omissions.

I am a parent of a pupil at the school and built it because I wanted to remove the pain of missing events in the emails.
